Chee adds a live evaluation chart beside your chess games on Chess.com and Lichess.org.

It runs Stockfish (one of the strongest chess engines) entirely in your browser using WebAssembly — no server, no account, no data sent anywhere.

• Eval chart — SVG area chart of the engine score at every move, with hover tooltip
• Adjustable search depth (15–22 ply)
• Catppuccin themes or match the site's look
• Draggable, resizable panel; Alt+C to hide/show
