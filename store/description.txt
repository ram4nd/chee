Chee reviews your Chess.com games once they are over: an evaluation chart of the whole game and a list of your mistakes, right above the move list.

While a game is in progress Chee shows nothing and does not load the engine, so it never gives you outside help during play.

It runs Stockfish (one of the strongest chess engines) entirely in your browser using WebAssembly: no server, no account, no data sent anywhere.

• Eval chart of every move, with hover scores and click-to-jump
• Your inaccuracies, mistakes and blunders, marked in the move list
• Click a mistake to see the position before it and find the better move yourself; reveal the engine's move when ready
• Search depth of 12, 15, 18 or 20
• Catppuccin themes or match the site's look
